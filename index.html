Visual Perception

Computer vision has long taken its questions from human vision, and this course teaches the two together: from filters and receptive fields to CNNs, Vision Transformers, self-supervised learning and generative models, with models tested against perceptual phenomena such as metamers and the Thatcher illusion. It ends with an original project: a working demo, or an experiment on the limits of today's vision systems.
