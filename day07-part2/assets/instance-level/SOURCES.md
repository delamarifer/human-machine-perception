# Figure sources for the instance-level deep dive

Figures are unchanged except for cropping and file format. Crops were found from the image (whitespace bands). Each paper keeps its own copyright, and each is credited on its slide. Paper figures come from each paper's arXiv source, except IPCL, which comes from the open-access article.

| file | source |
|---|---|
| `trial-1.png`, `trial-2.png`, `trial-3.png` | MOCHI repository, `assets/example_trial.png`, cut into three images with the text label removed. Bonnen et al. (2024). https://github.com/tzler/mochi_code (images CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) |
| `mochi-four-sets.jpg` | Bonnen et al. (2024), Evaluating Multiview Object Consistency in Humans and Image Models, example stimuli figure. https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.05862 |
| `models-easy.png`, `models-hard.png` | Bonnen et al. (2024), per-condition figure, panels `barense familiar_lowsim` and `barense abstract_hisim`. Accuracy is normalized: 0 is chance and 1 is ceiling. |
| `views-vs-image.png` | Bonnen, Malik, Kanazawa (2026), Human-level 3D shape perception emerges from multi-view learning, left panel of the main figure. https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17650 |
| `ipcl-method.png` | Konkle and Alvarez (2022), Nature Communications 13:491, Figure 1a (CC BY 4.0). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-28091-4 |
| `ipcl-brain.png` | Konkle and Alvarez (2022), Figure 2, the pOTC row (CC BY 4.0). |
| `crops.jpg`, `video-views.jpg` | Purushwalkam and Gupta (2020), Demystifying Contrastive Self-Supervised Learning, NeurIPS, augmentation figure and the top strip of the approach figure. https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.13916 |

The bar chart on "Some Conditions Are Hard for Everyone" is plotted in the slide from `mochi_code/assets/benchmark.csv`: mean `human_accuracy` per dataset and condition, with chance as 1 over the number of images in a trial.

The sketches on "Examples of What to Report" and "Example: One Dot Is One Trial" are drawings, not data.
