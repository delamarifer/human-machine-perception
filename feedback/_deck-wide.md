## Round 1 · 2026-10-26 · starting rules (from the animated-talk kit)
- Title first; one idea per click; a white lead-in before each new slide (`?blank=0` turns it off).
- Cues on screen, sentences in the notes; everything on screen is said aloud.
- One type scale (`h1.t`, `.statement`, `.question`, `.lead`, `.label`, `.cite`, `.popquote`). No ad-hoc sizes.
- No colored words. Data colors: slate-blue = eye to V1 and 3D interpretations; plum = ventral "what";
  pine = dorsal "where / how". Keep them consistent if later days reuse these ideas.
- Citations bottom-left, readable (`cite()`), black.
- Motion slower than feels right; dim the figure when a statement appears over it.
