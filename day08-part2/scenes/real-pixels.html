An image is a point

A real image, in gray levels: 256 × 256 pixels
Pick two of its pixels: one dark, one bright
Their two brightness values are the coordinates of one point
One axis per pixel: this image is one point in 65,536 dimensions. We can draw two
Add noise: a small random number on every pixel. For our two pixels, an arrow added to the point
Ten noise steps: ten arrows, one after another
100 steps: the image is mostly noise, and the point has wandered far
Image: Stable Diffusion 3.5 (VulcanSphere), Wikimedia Commons, public domain. Noise computed here: Gaussian, standard deviation 0.08 per step, on every pixel. After Welch Labs for 3Blue1Brown (2025)
