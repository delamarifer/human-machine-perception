An image is a point

A real image: here, in gray levels
Pick two of its pixels: one dark, one bright
Their brightness values are the coordinates of one point
Add a little noise: every pixel changes, and the point takes a step in a random direction
Ten noise steps: the point goes on a random walk
100 steps: the image is mostly noise, and the point has wandered far
Image: Stable Diffusion 3.5 (VulcanSphere), Wikimedia Commons, public domain. Noise computed here: Gaussian, 0.08 per step, on every pixel. After Welch Labs for 3Blue1Brown (2025)
