The network learns a field that changes with time

After 100 noise steps (t = 1): the arrows point to the middle of the data
After 1 noise step (t = 0.01): the arrows point to the spiral itself
So the network also takes the time t as an input
From t = 1 to 0: coarse field first, fine field at the end
On our spiral, the switch happens between t = 0.25 and t = 0.12
This field is the score: it points toward more likely, less noisy data
