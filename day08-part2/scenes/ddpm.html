One point, 100 steps: it jumps around, then lands on the spiral
256 points: chaos at first, then the spiral
Delete the random steps: the points crowd together, and the image stays a faded average until the very end
The network predicts an average. The random step turns it into a sample
Computed exactly: Ho et al. 2020, Algorithm 2, 100 steps, with and without its noise line, on the spiral and on a dataset of 12 images (ideal denoiser; it can only return one of its 12 images). Images: Wikimedia Commons, public domain and CC0. Toy example after Welch Labs for 3Blue1Brown (2025)
