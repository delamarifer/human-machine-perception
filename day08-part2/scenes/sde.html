Removing the random steps the right way

with random steps
dx = [f(x,t) − g(t)2 ∇ log pt(x)] dt + g(t) dw̄
no random steps
dx = [f(x,t) − ½ g(t)2 ∇ log pt(x)] dt
DDPM, written as one equation: a stochastic differential equation (SDE)
Model term: the move along the field
Random term: a random move
Added head to tail: the total move of the point in one step
No random term, half the field term: an ordinary differential equation (ODE)
With random steps: 256 points
No random steps, same starting points: the cloud passes through the same distributions and ends the same
One starting point, two different end points: only the cloud as a whole is the same
∇ log pt(x): the field (the score)  ·  f, g: set by the noise schedule  ·  dw̄: a random kick
Physics: the Fokker–Planck equation
