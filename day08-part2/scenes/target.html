Training, as written in the DDPM paper
Pick a clean training image x0: one point of the data
Pick a time t: here 12 of 100 noise steps
The 12 random steps add up to one draw of noise ε: jump there in one go
The network sees the noisy point and t, and must guess ε: the total noise
Why not just undo one step? That arrow is mostly the last random step
Many walks pass here. Their one-step arrows scatter; their average points to the data
The same walks’ total noise: arrows back to their own clean points
Same information on average, about 3 times less noise
At every point, the average arrow points back to the data: what εθ learns
