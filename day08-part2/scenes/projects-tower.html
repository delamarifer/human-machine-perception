Intuitive physics: will it fall?

Will this tower fall? Which way?
It falls to the right. People can tell at a glance, with no equations
One model: a rough physics simulation in the head, run many times from a blurry view
It falls in ? of 12 runs: a graded answer, not just yes or no
Another model: a deep network trained on many examples to predict what happens next
The question: which model predicts like people do, mistakes included?
