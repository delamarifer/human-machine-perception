no class class = cats new arrow
Leave the class out for some training examples: one network, two fields
Much noise: the two fields agree. Little noise: they part ways
One point: where each field sends it
The difference: from the tip of the gray arrow to the tip of the class arrow
Stretch the difference by α and use the new arrow
new = gray + α × (class − gray)
Computed exactly on the toy spiral; arrows in the full fields drawn at one length; t = step / 100. Ho and Salimans, Classifier-Free Diffusion Guidance, 2022 (they write w = α − 1). After Welch Labs for 3Blue1Brown (2025)
