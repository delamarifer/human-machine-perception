When judges do not pay attention

One person meets the court again and again: complied, or not
An attentive court: the outcome follows what they did
An inattentive court: the outcome ignores what they did
A learner who reasons perfectly. Attentive court: their sense of control stays high
Inattentive court: it falls. A loss of perceived control, the kind of helplessness relevant to trauma
No irrational thinking needed: in their experience, complying offered no protection
The harm comes from the court’s inattention, not from a flaw in the person
