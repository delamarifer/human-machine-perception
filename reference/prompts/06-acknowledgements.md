# Prompt 6: acknowledgements slides

Acknowledgements change until the last minute and are personal. Give them their own session.

---

Build the acknowledgements as their own part (`thanks`). My people, groups and photos are in
`<path to a .pptx, folder or list>`; that file is the source of truth for who is included, the
grouping and the spelling.

- Slides: photos only, grouped (advisor, committee, mentors, lab, friends, family…), titles in the
  deck's title style, tidy rows or collages. No names on the slides, except: <list the slides
  that keep names, e.g. committee and mentors>.
- Names go in the presenter notes, one passage per photo click, in my order.
- My written script for each person is in `<path>`. Paste it word for word; don't edit my wording,
  pronouns or punctuation. Where I haven't written anything yet, leave `[your words for X]`.
- People without a photo come off the slides; keep them in the notes so I still thank them aloud.
- When I send a photo without saying who it is, don't guess: put `[who?]` in the notes and ask me.
- Push when done, and check in the presenter view that every passage lands on its click.
