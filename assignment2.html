Assignment 2 · 15%

Two other projects

After the talks, pick two projects by other teams. Answer the same questions about each one. A few sentences is enough. Use something specific from the talk: a number, a figure, an example, or a failure.

Weight15% of the grade
WhenAfter the talks
ChooseTwo projects, not yours

How it works

Listen during the talks. You cannot answer these from the title alone.

The two projects must be by other teams, not your own and not a teammate's. Name each project so we know which talk you mean.

Answer questions 1 to 5 once for the first project, then again for the second. Then answer question 6 once, about both.

Questions

Same five questions for each project.

  1. The idea

    In one sentence, what were they trying to find out or build, and why should someone care?

  2. Setup

    What data did they use, which models, and what did they measure?

  3. Results

    What was the main finding, in one sentence? What evidence did they show?

  4. Where it fails

    What does not work, and what can their setup not tell you?

  5. What it means

    What does this say about how models see, or about how people see?

  6. The two together

    Where do the two projects agree, and where do they not? Name one thing you would test next.

What we look for

Each answer should be specific to that talk. Restating the project title in new words is not enough.

Questions 1 to 5 are about what the team said and showed. Question 6 is your comparison, and it has to use both projects.
